🔒

Private, no server

Your holdings are encrypted on your phone (AES-256), and backups are encrypted with your password before they reach your own Google Drive. We run no server that can see your data.

🕌

Zakat

Gold nisab (85 g of 24K) and silver nisab (595 g), your hawl due date and a reminder, all valued at the latest available price with its time and source shown. The result is a guide; ask a scholar about special cases.

💱

Many currencies

Record a bar bought in riyals and another in pounds or dollars; the app separates the metal's gain from exchange-rate changes.
