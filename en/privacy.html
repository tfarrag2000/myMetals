myMetals Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Applies to the Android app and the web version

myMetals keeps your data on your device. We run no server that receives your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Data you enter

  • Your name, your gold, silver and other metal holdings, cash, sales, family member names and zakat settings.
  • Stored on your device only. On Android they are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore). The web version keeps them in browser storage, which is less protected than the Android app.
  • PIN: the PIN itself is never stored, only a value derived from it (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with a random salt).
  • Fingerprint: checked by the phone's system; the app never receives fingerprint data.

2. Google account and backup (optional)

  • If you link Google Drive, the app gets your name, email address and access to its own hidden app folder only (drive.appdata). It cannot see any other file in your account.
  • Backups are encrypted on your device with a password you choose before upload (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, then AES-256-GCM). Neither Google nor we can read them.
  • The vault password (for automatic backups) and the recovery key are kept on your device only, in secure storage, and are deleted when you wipe the app's data.
  • Family sharing files, PDF reports and Excel files are created on your device and shared by you. A PDF report can be protected with a password.
  • Family sharing can also be shown as a QR code on your screen for your partner to scan. The code holds the same encrypted package and goes from screen to camera directly, through no server.

3. Internet use

The app connects over HTTPS only for the following, and none of these requests contain information about you or your holdings:

  • Public metal prices and exchange rates: from api.gold-api.com and open.er-api.com, with api.exchangerate-api.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net as fallbacks.
  • Past prices (the calculator, purchase dates and zakat payments): daily tables from cdn.jsdelivr.net or currency-api.pages.dev, and exchange rates before 2024 from api.frankfurter.app.
  • Checking for a new version: the GitHub and web versions read latest.json from this site. A copy installed from Google Play asks Google Play instead, and is updated by Google Play.
  • Backup and restore with Google Drive, if you turn it on.

As with any website, these services see your device's IP address when it connects to them.

4. Background checks and notifications (Android)

  • If you turn on a gold price alert or the zakat reminder, the app fetches public prices about every 15 minutes while closed and shows a notification on your device when needed.
  • All of this happens on the device. Your alert thresholds and zakat date are never sent anywhere.
  • The check stops when you turn off the alert and the reminder.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

6. Android permissions

  • INTERNET: prices, backups and checking for new versions.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: unlocking the vault with your fingerprint, if you turn it on.
  • CAMERA: scanning a family sharing QR code and photographing an invoice. Requested only when you use one of them; the pictures stay on your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: price alert and zakat reminder notifications. Requested only when you turn one of them on.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → “Reset & Wipe All Data on This Device”, then type DELETE to confirm.
  • In Android: Settings → Apps → myMetals → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web version: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Backups in Drive: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → myMetals → Delete hidden app data.

We hold no copy of your data to delete, because it never reaches us.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from children.
